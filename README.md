# 📊 End-to-End Retail Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
This project presents an end-to-end analysis of retail sales data to uncover trends, identify top-performing products, and generate actionable business insights. The analysis focuses on sales patterns, regional performance, and key drivers of revenue.

## Objective
To analyze retail sales data and answer key business questions related to revenue, seasonality, product performance, and regional differences.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* Total Revenue: ₹2297200.8603
* Average Order Value: ₹229.85800083049827
* Top Performing Region: West

## Key Insights
* Sales exhibit consistent growth with periodic fluctuations
* A small group of products drives a large portion of total revenue
* Regional differences significantly influence overall sales performance
* Higher discounts tend to reduce profitability

## Business Recommendations
* Focus on high-performing products and categories to maximize revenue
* Optimize discount strategies to protect profit margins
* Plan inventory and promotions around peak sales periods
* Improve strategies in lower-performing regions
